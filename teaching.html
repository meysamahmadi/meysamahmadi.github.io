Teaching Philosophy

I believe engineering education is most effective when students understand not only how to apply an equation or analysis method, but also the physical reasoning behind it. My goal is to connect theoretical concepts with practical engineering problems and encourage students to question, interpret, and communicate their results.

In geotechnical engineering, many problems involve uncertainty and incomplete knowledge of subsurface conditions. I therefore value teaching approaches that emphasize engineering judgment, visualization, and the connection between measurements, physical behavior, and design decisions.
